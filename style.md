# My video style

Read this before every edit or new film.

Colours: each film has its own palette. The website is black, white, and gray. A code film may be night and cyan. A science film may be warm paper. A story may be night and gold. Do not reuse one palette for two films.

Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque for display. JetBrains Mono for code. Kalam for Devanagari.

Captions: two or three words at a time, lower third, white on a dark film or ink on a paper film. No box behind the words. No em dash in spoken or on-screen text.

Pacing: one continuous voice. Do not cut the narration into two-word bursts. A new picture enters when the sentence reaches it. Pictures move: they arrive, travel, and leave. A film is one take, not a stack of title cards.

Music: none under these showcase films unless a bed is measured and ducked under the voice.

Always: the voice says the words that are on screen. State the problem, what we are trying to do, then the solution. Facts on screen come from a trace, a measurement, or a line the creator wrote.

Never: copyrighted characters, a silent film on the site, pill captions, pink and purple chrome on the website, two voices playing at once.

Tools, when an edit needs them: FFmpeg to cut and measure, ElevenLabs through the voice adapter for speech and word times, HyperFrames to draw and render. A style correction gets added here.
